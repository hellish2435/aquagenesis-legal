# AI-Generated Content Disclosure

**Last updated: 2026-10-04**

## A quick note from the developer

This game is being built with a lot of AI assistance right now, especially for code and artwork. The actual game idea, major systems, progression, mechanics, balancing, and overall direction are things I've been designing myself, with AI helping me turn those ideas into something playable while I learn.

As I get better at development and art, I want to gradually replace more of the AI-made stuff with my own work and make the game feel more and more like something fully created by me.

— Kenneth Pennell, Porchlight Studios

## What is made with AI

- **Art:** almost all of the Game's art is generated with **OpenAI** image generation (through ChatGPT) from my descriptions, then chosen and put together by me. This includes the menus and buttons, backgrounds, food, eggs, Professor Shelly, level badges and icons.
- **Code and in-game text:** the Game's code and its text (menus, tutorials, Shelly's lines, achievements and species facts) are written with **Claude Code** (Anthropic) from my designs, and I review them.

## What is not made with AI

- The game idea, its systems, progression, mechanics, balancing and overall direction are my own design.

## While you play

Nothing is generated by AI while you play. All of the Game's content was made before it reached you.
