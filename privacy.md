# Aquagenesis Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-03**

> **DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Replace everything in [brackets] before publishing.

This policy explains what information **[STUDIO NAME]** ("we", "us") collects when you play **Aquagenesis** (the "Game"), how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- **Playing offline needs no account.** Your aquarium, fish and settings are saved only on your device.
- **Going online is optional** and only for players aged **13 and up**. If you go online, we store a small profile so other players can see it.
- We **don't sell** your information, we **don't show ads**, and we **don't use tracking or analytics** tools.
- You can **delete your online account** at any time from inside the Game.

## 1. Information stored only on your device

When you play, the Game saves your progress (tanks, fish, items, settings and photos you take) on your device. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to us. Deleting the Game or its save files removes it.

The Game also keeps on your device:

- a copy of these legal documents, so you can read them offline;
- if you go online: a sign-in token, so you stay signed in;
- your answer to the age question, as a simple yes/no (we **don't** keep your birth date).

## 2. Information we collect if you go online

Going online is optional. If you choose it, we collect:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| An account ID created for you | To run your account |
| Your email address (only if you add one) | So you can sign in again and recover your account |
| Your password (only if you set one) | To sign you in. It is stored scrambled ("hashed") by our provider; we can't read it. |
| Your public profile card: display name, profile picture choice, title, badges, showcase records (e.g. level, Fishpedia count) and friend code | So other players can see your profile |
| Which version of our Terms and this policy you accepted, and when, plus confirmation that you're 13 or older | To keep a record of your agreement |
| Technical information our provider records when the Game connects, such as IP address, device/app details and timestamps | To keep the service secure and working |

## 3. Reading the legal documents

When you open a legal document in the Game, the Game downloads its latest version from GitHub (where the documents are published). Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address when this happens. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## 4. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- create and run your online account and show your profile to other players;
- let you sign in, recover and delete your account;
- keep the Game and our services secure and prevent abuse;
- send you account emails you ask for (such as confirming your email address). We don't send marketing emails.

**Legal bases (for players in the EU/UK):** providing the service you asked for (contract), keeping it secure (legitimate interests), and complying with the law.

## 5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the Game:

- **Supabase** (accounts and database), hosted in the **United States**: https://supabase.com/privacy
- **GitHub** (publishing these legal documents): https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Other players can see your **public profile card** (section 2). We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect players and the Game.

## 6. Where your information is stored

Online account information is stored in the United States. If you live elsewhere (for example in the EU or UK), your information is transferred there with the safeguards our providers offer.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Online account information:** until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your login, profile and acceptance records from our live database right away; copies may remain in routine backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.
- **Information on your device:** until you delete it or the Game.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account:** Settings → Account → Delete online account.
- **Stop being online:** Settings → Account → Sign out. Your offline game is kept.
- **Change your profile:** edit it in the Game and tap "Update my online profile".
- Depending on where you live, you may have the right to **access, correct, delete or receive a copy** of your information, or to **object** to how we use it. Contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
- **California residents:** we don't sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by California law.

## 9. Children

Online features are for players **13 and older**. Before going online, the Game asks for your birth month and year; if you're under 13, online features stay off on that device and we don't collect any information from you. If we learn that we have information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## 10. Security

We use our providers' security features (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, rules that let each player change only their own profile). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the "Last updated" date above, and the Game always shows the latest version. If the changes are important, players who are online will be asked to read and accept the new version.
