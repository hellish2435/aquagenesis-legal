# Your Data — Requests

**Last updated: 2026-10-04**

You can see, change or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time.

## In the game

Go to **Settings → Account**:

- **Update my online profile** changes what other players can see.
- **Delete online account** erases your login, profile and records from our servers straight away. Your game on your device is kept.

## By email

Email **aquagenesis.support@gmail.com** from the address on your account (or include your friend code if you never added an email) and tell us what you'd like:

- a **copy** of the information we hold about you;
- a **correction**;
- **deletion** of your account and information;
- anything else about your privacy rights.

We'll reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you before we act on it.

See our [Privacy Policy](privacy) for what we collect and why.
